Privacy notice

How SignaCheck AI handles your signatures

Last updated: . This notice describes what the current version of the service actually does.

Development version. The verification server and AI model aren't connected yet. Right now, the images you select stay in your browser and aren't sent anywhere. We'll update this notice before the server goes live.

1. What we process

  • Two signature images you choose: a reference signature and a questioned signature.
  • Basic file details your browser reads in order to validate them: file type, file size, and image dimensions.

We don't ask for your name, email address, ID documents, or an account. Please don't upload images that contain other personal information, such as full documents or ID cards. Crop the image to the signature only.

2. Where your images go

Currently: your images are read and previewed entirely in your browser. Because no verification server is connected yet, no image is uploaded to us or to any third party.

When the verification server is enabled, both images will be sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server for analysis. We'll update this section to describe exactly how the server processes them and how long it keeps them before that change takes effect.

3. Storage and retention

  • We don't store your images in any database.
  • Previews exist only in your browser's memory for this page. They disappear when you click Remove or Clear all, or when you close or reload the page.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.
  • If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It holds only the word "light" or "dark" and is never sent to us.

4. External services

No external AI provider or third-party service receives your images. If that ever changes, we'll name the provider here and ask for your consent first.

5. How we protect files

  • Files are checked for supported formats (JPG, PNG, WebP), a 5 MB size limit, and dimension limits before they're accepted.
  • Analysis requires your explicit consent on the verification page.
  • When server processing is introduced, it will include server-side content validation, rate limiting, and automatic deletion of temporary files.

6. Your rights and deletion

Because we don't currently store any images, there's nothing for us to delete. You can remove your images from the page at any time. If you have questions about your data, contact us at privacy@signacheck.example.

We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable data-protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

7. Limitations of the service

SignaCheck AI provides a preliminary, automated comparison. It can't prove a person's identity or establish that a signature is genuine. For legal or financial decisions, consult a qualified forensic document examiner.
