How it works

Three simple steps

You need at least one signature you know is genuine. Without a trusted reference, there's nothing reliable to compare against.

1

Upload a reference

Add a clear photo or scan of a signature you know was genuinely written by the person.

2

Upload the questioned signature

Add the signature you want to check. Clear, well-lit, flat images give the most reliable comparison.

3

Review the assessment

You'll get one of three results: consistent with reference, potential mismatch, or inconclusive. Each comes with an explanation and its limitations.

Features

Designed to be clear about what it knows

Every result tells you what was compared, how confident the comparison is, and where its limits are.

Reference-based comparison

Compares the questioned signature against a signature you trust, not against a generic idea of what a signature should look like.

Honest "inconclusive" results

If an image is unclear or the evidence isn't strong enough, you'll get an inconclusive result, not a guess.

Image quality checks

Warns you when an image is too small, unsupported, or unlikely to give a reliable comparison.

No account needed

No sign-up and no profile. You only provide the two images needed for the comparison.

Privacy & security

Signatures are sensitive. We treat them that way.

A handwritten signature is personal information. SignaCheck AI is designed to collect as little as possible and keep it for as short a time as possible.

Read the privacy notice
  • Only the two images you choose are processed. No names, ID documents, or accounts.
  • Images are checked for valid type and size before they're used.
  • You must give explicit consent before any analysis.
  • You can clear your images and results from the page at any time.
  • The privacy notice describes exactly what currently happens to your images.

Important limitations. SignaCheck AI gives a preliminary, automated assessment. It can't prove who wrote a signature, it won't detect every forgery, and a "potential mismatch" doesn't mean a signature is forged. People's signatures naturally vary. For legal, financial, or disputed matters, consult a qualified forensic document examiner.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can SignaCheck AI prove a signature is genuine?

No. It compares the visual characteristics of two signature images and gives a preliminary assessment. Visual similarity alone can't establish identity or authenticity.

Why do I need a reference signature?

Everyone signs differently. To judge whether a signature is consistent with a person's handwriting, you need a sample you know that person genuinely wrote. Without one, a reliable person-specific comparison isn't possible.

What does "inconclusive" mean?

The system doesn't have enough evidence to make an assessment. Common causes are blurry, very small, or cropped images, or signatures that are too short or too simple.

Is the similarity score a probability that the signature is genuine?

No. When shown, the score measures how similar the model considers the two images. It isn't the probability that a signature is genuine or forged.

What image formats can I upload?

JPG, PNG, and WebP images up to 5 MB each. For best results, use a dark pen on plain white paper, photographed flat and in good light.

Do you store my signatures?

See the privacy notice. It describes exactly what currently happens to uploaded images and how long they're kept.
