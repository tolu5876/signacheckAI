Image quality warnings

    Limitations

      Tips for a clear image

      • Use a dark pen on plain white paper.
      • Photograph straight on, flat, and in even light. Avoid shadows.
      • Crop close to the signature, leaving a small margin.
      • Avoid lines, stamps, or printed text behind the signature.
      • Use an image at least 300 pixels wide.

      File requirements

      • Formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
      • Max size: 5 MB per image
      • Dimensions: 100×40 to 6000×6000 pixels

      Preliminary only. Results are automated screening assessments. They can't prove who wrote a signature and don't replace a forensic document examiner.
